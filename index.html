RTT/3 — Topology Engine

Structural Topology • Regime Intersections • Drift‑Coherence Overlays

Purpose

RTT/3 constructs topology surfaces, regime intersections, and drift‑coherence overlays from RTT/2 structural, drift, and coherence geometry. It is the topology bridge between RTT/2 and RTT/12 in the TriadicFrameworks canon.

Pipeline Position

OpenGPU → RTT/1 → RTT/2 → RTT/3 → RTT/12

Operators

  • topology-map — structural topology from regimeView
  • topology-scan — drift/coherence intersection analysis
  • topology-geometry — topology surfaces from coherence geometry
  • gpu-topology — RTT/3 GPU topology (surfaces, intersections, overlays)

GPU Topology

The gpu-topology operator consumes RTT/2 gpu-stack output and produces GPU topology surfaces, regime intersections, and drift‑coherence overlays. It is the RTT/3 integration point for OpenGPU.
